# Responsible Use and Safety

ZhiTan is designed to help people understand their own experience, research roles, prepare for interviews, and make informed job-search decisions. It is not designed to act without the user's awareness or to misrepresent a candidate.

## Required boundaries

- Keep a human in control of every external action. Review generated resumes, outreach, answers, and summaries before use.
- Use only accounts and data you are authorized to access.
- Respect website terms, robots policies, rate limits, security checks, and requests to stop.
- Treat resumes, job-search history, transcripts, screenshots, and model prompts as sensitive personal data.
- Clearly distinguish facts supplied by the user from model suggestions. Do not fabricate employers, dates, projects, skills, credentials, or outcomes.

## Prohibited uses

- Capturing or exporting passwords, cookies, session tokens, private messages, or unrelated personal information.
- Bypassing CAPTCHAs, anti-bot controls, access restrictions, paid content, or security verification.
- Sending applications, outreach, or messages without the user's explicit review and action.
- Impersonating another person or creating deceptive candidate profiles.
- Automatically writing or submitting answers on assessment platforms, accessing hidden tests, evading proctoring, or concealing assistance.
- Recording or transcribing people without the notice or consent required by applicable law and policy.
- High-volume scraping or messaging that creates abuse, spam, or unreasonable load.

## Browser assistant

The browser assistant should request the narrowest permissions possible and operate only after a user gesture. It may read supported visible pages needed for the selected task. It must not export browser credentials. Assessment capture is read-only: generated code stays in ZhiTan and is never written back to or submitted on the third-party page.

## Model output

AI output can be incomplete, biased, or wrong. ZhiTan should expose enough context for the user to review important claims. Do not use model output as the sole basis for employment, legal, medical, financial, or other high-impact decisions.

Report security vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Report safety design concerns in a regular issue only after removing private or exploitable details.
