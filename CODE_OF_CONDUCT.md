# Code of Conduct

ZhiTan is committed to a respectful, practical, and inclusive contributor community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when discussing code, design, and product decisions.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome questions from contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's resume, contact details, account data, private messages, or security information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and project direction without harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, impersonation, and deliberate misuse of project channels are not tolerated. This also includes encouraging unsafe automation, credential theft, assessment cheating, or deceptive job-search practices.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or another private channel they publish. Do not expose sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and focused on protecting affected people and the project community.
